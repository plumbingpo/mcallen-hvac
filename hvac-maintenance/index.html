Warning Signs for HVAC Maintenance

The sooner you notice these red flags, the easier and cheaper the problem is to resolve. These are the signals our technicians see most often when a problem is underway:

  • No real sense of how healthy your heating and cooling equipment is
  • Cooling or heating that takes noticeably longer to reach the setpoint

Maintenance Tips for HVAC Maintenance

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly heating and cooling problems down the road. Put these technician-approved steps to work around your property:

  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of vegetation and rinse the coil gently each spring
  • Pay attention to new noises or longer cycles and mention them at your next visit
  • Get your tune-ups on the calendar during mild weather, well before the rush
  • Flush the condensate line every spring so algae cannot build up and clog it
  • Replace your filter on a one- to three-month cycle; nothing else you do pays off more
  • Test carbon monoxide detectors monthly if any of your equipment burns fuel
  • Leave every supply and return register open, including those in unused rooms
